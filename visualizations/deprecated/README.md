# Parked work

Nothing here is one of the eight animations, and nothing here is imported or rendered by them.
It is kept because it may be wanted later, and because what these scenes measured is part of the
record. Paths in these files were repointed when the folders moved but they have not been
re-rendered since, so treat them as needing a look before use.

**`draft_equivalence.py` is finished, not abandoned.** It is fully narrated and timed (about two
minutes), and it is the defence of animation #1: the argument that a value-ordered snake draft
reduces exactly to random drafting, so the Z-score scene's premise is not a convenient fiction.
It is here only because it is not on the list of eight. Say the word and it becomes a ninth
folder. Its prep is `prepare_draft_equivalence_data.py`; its narration is still inline, in the
scene file, rather than in a `narration.py`.

The rest are superseded:

- **`seed_landscape.py`** — the first attempt at the multi-start picture, on a synthetic
  landscape. Replaced by `7_seed_menu`, which measures the real objective instead.
- **`category_gradient.py`** — a gradient walk over the punting curves. Replaced by
  `8_gradient_descent`. It imports from `5_punting/punting.py` (the import is repointed, but the
  two have since diverged).
- **`payoff_formats.py`** — the three scoring formats side by side (Most Categories, Each
  Category, Rotisserie). Parked on the call that each format deserves its own video rather than
  a comparison. Its measurement stands and is worth keeping: all three formats punt exactly
  three categories, and what differs is what the punt is WORTH — Most Categories +9.5% over
  perfect balance, Each Category +2.8%, Rotisserie +1.1%.
